Timer House privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

The Timer House uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Timer House YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • It signs in with the channel owner's Google account through Google's own sign-in, and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload Timer House videos, set their thumbnails, add them to playlists and read their view counts.
  • It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else. The sign-in key is kept only on the owner's own computer.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Watching Timer House videos on YouTube is covered by Google's privacy policy.
